## Main point

BiSAIL reports `0.76–0.77` mean task-success rates across three language-feedback variation classes by compiling language and vision into a black-box trajectory objective plus a coordination class and optimizing a composed diffusion distribution at inference, but this changes the motion-generation path and does not isolate either an executable training reward or a reference oracle under Sam's fixed trainer.

## Mechanism

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    L[Inputs: language l, image o,<br/>end-effector/object keypoints] --> E[ESA-CoT<br/>black-box R(tau;l,o)<br/>+ one of 6 coordination classes]
    B[FROZEN online: pretrained BMP p_phi<br/>task-agnostic motion proposals] --> S[Reward-scored diffusion search]
    E --> S
    S --> M[Compositional MCMC modulation]
    P[FROZEN online: offline task DP p_theta<br/>task-compatibility score] --> M
    E --> M
    M --> T[Output: adapted dual-arm<br/>pose/gripper trajectory tau*]
    T --> X[Robot execution]
    X --> O[Feedback: post-execution image/context]
    O --> C[Same MLLM self-critic<br/>revised R' + success delta]
    C --> E
    F[Sam freezes: scene, observations, actions,<br/>dynamics, controller, tracking reward, budget] -. BiSAIL is not a drop-in two-knob change .-> M
```

## What transfers to Sam's harness

- Translate language into a typed proposal before execution: intended change, required state/object fields, candidate reward or oracle edit, coordination/reference mode, and predicted metric effect.
- Keep the translator separate from the executable artifact. BiSAIL's `89.8%` executability leaves a measured fail-closed validation need.
- Treat mode labels as a closed vocabulary with explicit semantics. A future reference oracle can map each accepted label to immutable references, guards, transitions, and recovery behavior.
- Feed post-run objective evidence back into proposal generation, but let an independent evaluator—not the generator—decide promotion or termination.
- Compare steering on held-out language/visual variations and report artifact validity, intent alignment, task success, transition/coordination quality, retries, and end-to-end latency.

## What does not transfer

- BiSAIL does not select, crop, order, splice, or certify immutable reference clips; it synthesizes a new action trajectory through inference-time diffusion.
- Its black-box `R(tau;l,o)` ranks complete candidate trajectories during sampling. It is not a bounded per-step executable task reward consumed by Sam's unchanged RL trainer.
- Adding a task-agnostic diffusion prior, requiring score access to a task-specific diffusion policy, and replacing the action-generation path violate Sam's fixed controller/observation/action boundary.
- Cross-embodiment experiments retrain BMP, so they do not establish portability of one pinned oracle, reward, controller, or reference artifact.
- The paper does not validate dynamic adaptation, objective-reward calibration, reward-hacking resistance, safe generated-code execution, or RL-Sculptor implementation.

## Failure modes and caveats

- **Reported limitation:** adaptation is kinematic only; speed, acceleration, and interaction-force modulation are excluded.
- **Measured generated-reward failure:** full ESA-CoT's ER is `89.8%`, so about one in ten benchmark outputs is not programmatically executable; the differentiable-reward variant reaches only `42.9%` ER.
- **Non-independent stop rule:** the same ESA-CoT/MLLM revises `R` and emits success bit `delta`; no independent gate validates the stop decision.
- **Proposal-prior failure:** task-DP samples overfit demonstrations; increasing noise or weakening observation conditioning produces jerky or physically implausible motion.
- **Composition failure:** classifier guidance degrades as `g` rises (`300` to `500`), removing MCMC sharply reduces task/coordination scores, and removing BMP noise collapses intent alignment to `32.3%`.
- **Reproduction gap:** essential sampler weights/schedules, retry cap, reward schema, exact prompt/model snapshot, task-policy training contract, and evaluation uncertainty definition are absent.
- **Latency gap:** `0.16 s` is BMP sampling only; MLLM reasoning, iterative modulation, robot execution, reflection, and retries have no end-to-end timing.
- **Release contradiction:** arXiv v1 says code is available, but the latest official repository has only site/media assets and a placeholder “Code (Coming soon)” link.
