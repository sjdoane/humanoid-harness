## Main point

With the per-domain SAC/SimbaV2 trainer held fixed, the two-task ablation reports that adding trajectory-level visual diagnosis to checkpoint warm-start raises alignment from `0.35` to `0.82` and success from `0.55` to `0.83`; this supports executable task-reward search, not reference composition, runtime oracle logic, or a common-initialization reward-only comparison.

## Mechanism

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    I[INPUT<br/>task instruction I<br/>environment code + image E] --> T[GPT-5<br/>ordered subtask list T^i]
    T --> G[GPT-5<br/>N executable reward candidates R_n^i]
    F[FROZEN PER DOMAIN<br/>environment/MDP E<br/>SAC + SimbaV2 learner L<br/>steps, updates, score procedure] --> P[Train policy pi_n^i]
    G --> P
    P --> K[Roll out K=3 episodes<br/>frames + reward-component logs]
    K --> A[GPT-5 feedback<br/>subtask scores {0,.5,1}<br/>rationales + failure summary]
    A --> B[Mean subtask score<br/>select arg max candidate]
    B --> U[Revise at most one subtask<br/>then revise reward code]
    U -->|next-generation feedback| G
    B -->|optional best-checkpoint warm-start| P
    B --> O[OUTPUT after M=5<br/>best executable task reward]
    P --> Q[SEPARATE REPORTING<br/>GPT-4.1 instruction score<br/>+ benchmark binary success]
```

## Public implementation boundary

- The pinned author repository publishes 16 environment-dependent reward-function text fragments (`compute_dense_reward` for 12 ManiSkill tasks and `get_reward` for 4 HumanoidBench tasks), policy videos, and the project site. It does not publish the RDA generation, compilation/validation, rollout, VLM-analysis, selection, checkpoint, or training implementation. The public artifacts therefore verify example reward outputs, not an end-to-end runnable RDA system.

## What transfers to Sam's harness

- Treat `task_reward_k` as immutable executable code with a typed simulator-state input contract, bounded named components, explicit weights, a scalar output, and a source hash.
- Freeze the environment, observation/action contract, SAC/SimbaV2 trainer, per-candidate budget, rollout sampler, and candidate score while comparing reward revisions. Record requested settings separately from observed worker facts.
- Keep search feedback separate from objective reporting. RDA's search uses GPT-5 subtask scores and rationales; final reporting uses GPT-4.1 instruction scores plus benchmark success. Sam still needs independent trust, contact, naturalness, and dynamics gates.
- Version the subtask rubric as search state. Because RDA edits that rubric, rescore candidates under one immutable objective evaluator before comparing across iterations or promoting a reward.
- Persist the complete revision lineage: instruction, environment bytes, subtask list, VLM snapshot, reward code, compile/validation result, policy initialization, rollout frames, component logs, scores, rationales, selected candidate, and resulting checkpoint.
- Use visual and component-level diagnostics to propose a narrow reward edit, then retrain and re-evaluate. Do not treat the diagnostic model's explanation as proof of causality.
- Either disable cross-iteration checkpoint warm-start for a reward-only comparison or bind the exact initialization/checkpoint lineage as an explicit experimental factor. The paper's own ablation shows warm-start changes outcomes.
- Compile generated code only inside a fail-closed, resource-bounded validation and execution boundary. The paper states candidates are compiled and validated but does not define a safety contract.

## What does not transfer

- RDA does not compose reference clips, select motion modes, construct a phase-window automaton, or implement closed-loop oracle logic.
- Its subtask list is design-time text used to generate and judge rewards. The learned policy is not shown to observe a subtask or playback clock; explicit subtask-state conditioning is proposed as future work.
- Warm-starting from the previous best policy means the full loop is not evidence for a task-reward-only intervention under identical policy initialization.
- GPT-4.1 pointwise video ratings are a model-based proxy, not a calibrated human or physical ground truth. No human agreement study is reported.
- Results do not establish real-robot transfer, portability to a different controller/trainer, or robustness to changed observations, dynamics, scene, or budget.
- The public repository is not sufficient to reproduce RDA, and no result implies that RL-Sculptor implements it.

## Failure modes and caveats

- **Reported limitation:** Every added reward candidate requires a full RL training run; performance rises with candidate count, making search costly.
- **Reported limitation:** Diagnostics inherit VLM context limits, inference cost, and imperfect fine-grained visual reasoning.
- **Reported failure:** Earlier and later subtask rewards can conflict. In Humanoid Basketball, catching reward suppresses exploration of throwing; runtime subtask-state conditioning is future work.
- **Observed behavior:** An early package policy attains the goal by leaning its torso into the box rather than using both hands. RDA's diagnostic loop later gates forward progress on bilateral contact.
- **Causal caveat:** Optional best-checkpoint warm-start couples candidate reward and policy initialization. The `+Ckpt` ablation confirms a measurable effect.
- **Selection caveat:** The scoring procedure is fixed, but the subtask text being scored can change. The paper does not specify rescoring all candidates under one rubric before returning the best reward across iterations.
- **Metric caveat:** A 1–5 score divided by five has support `[0.2,1]`, although the paper calls the normalized range `[0,1]`. The evaluator is pointwise because of visual-context limits.
- **Source inconsistency:** Sec. 5.1 gives `2048` ManiSkill environments; Supplement Table 1 gives `2000`.
- **Source inconsistency:** Package text says success after the second iteration and alignment `0.68` by iteration 4; the Figure 6 caption says success after the first and `0.68` at the fifth iteration.
- **Source inconsistency:** Supplement C labels `1–2` ManiSkill and `6–8` HumanoidBench GPU-hours as per iteration, but five such iterations do not yield the stated `20–40` and `240–360` total ranges.
- **Reproduction and safety gap:** The paper does not specify compilation/validation checks or generated-code isolation, and the public repository omits the search/training implementation.
