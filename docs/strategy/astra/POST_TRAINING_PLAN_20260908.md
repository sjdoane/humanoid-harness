## Research target

```text
INPUT: task intent + available references + initial reward + development evidence
                                     |
                   human or harness revises O and/or r
                                     |
                   supplied VIBE post-training process
                                     |
                OUTPUT: policy + rollouts + video + effort
                                     |
                    independent task evaluation
                                     |
                     diagnosis -> next revision

Frozen per comparison: scene/MDP, initial controller, allowed trainable set,
training method/budget, feedback access and evaluator. Final held-out outcomes
do not enter the revision path. The given trainer updates permitted weights.
```

- Dynamic tasks require consequential impacts, object motion or support changes.

  harness revision. Same feedback types and matched training budgets.
