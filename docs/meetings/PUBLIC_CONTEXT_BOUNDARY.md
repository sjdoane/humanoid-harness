# Public meeting-context boundary

**Status:** sanitized repository record  
**Evidence class:** user-authorized project direction, not literature evidence

## Admitted public direction

- This repository starts a new research line focused on oracle/reference
  composition for humanoid control.
- The prior RL-Sculptor workspace is a read-only historical source.
- The intended evaluation target is Gymnasium Humanoid, including complex,
  phase-aware transitions and recovery-oriented failure cases.
- Literature claims and implementation claims must remain separate.

These points restate the user's current repository instructions. They do not
purport to reproduce a meeting transcript or a collaborator's complete views.

## Intentionally excluded

- raw meeting transcripts;
- private diagrams or attachments;
- contact or account information;
- unpublished system details, including private VIBE material;
- statements attributed to a collaborator without a public citable source or
  explicit approval for publication.

No raw private meeting artifact was copied into this repository. The legacy
knowledge-graph snapshot can mention the existence of private context as a
boundary, but it does not contain the underlying transcript or diagrams.

## Future handling

Keep raw private notes outside the public repository. Add only a compact public
decision record after human review, with the decision date, accountable owner,
scope, and rationale. A public paper claim still requires a registered primary
source and cannot inherit authority from a meeting note.

## Limitation

This migration cannot establish that all research or knowledge from prior
conversations was captured. It preserves only the locally available, sanitized
artifacts listed in the migration manifest.
